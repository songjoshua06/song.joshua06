Project

Mechanical Arm — Industrial Build

The start of a ground-up rebuild of a mechanical arm for industrial use. I'm beginning with the gripper — currently built as a single degree-of-freedom mechanism, with a second DOF next — and working toward a full 7-DOF arm.

Role: Hardware & mechanical build lead Team: Multidisciplinary, with master's students Tools: Onshape, CAD Status: In progress — gripper rebuild Target: 7-DOF arm

01 · GRIPPER

Rebuilding the gripper

I'm rebuilding the arm from the ground up for industrial use, starting at the business end: the gripper. The current design is a single degree-of-freedom mechanism — a geared linkage that drives both jaws together from one actuator. It's the first subsystem in the rebuild, and getting the gripper's motion and geometry right is the foundation for everything mounted behind it.

This is a multidisciplinary project — I'm currently working alongside a group of master's students to build it. I lead the hardware and mechanical side, owning the design and physical build of the gripper and arm, while the software and control work is handled by the master's students.

02 · WHAT'S NEXT

Toward a full 7-DOF arm

  • Gripper — now1 DOF
  • Gripper — next2 DOF
  • Full arm — target7 DOF

Next, the gripper moves from one degree of freedom to two, adding a second independent motion for more control over how it grasps. From there the build scales up to the full arm — a 7-DOF design that gives it the reach and dexterity of an industrial manipulator. This page will grow as each stage comes together.
