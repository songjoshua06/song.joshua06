Project

Mechanical Arm — Industrial Build

A ground-up rebuild of a mechanical arm for industrial use. I'm starting at the gripper — now on its second iteration, a three-point claw — and building toward housings, embedded tech, a second degree of freedom, and ultimately a full 7-DOF arm.

Role: Hardware & mechanical build lead Team: Multidisciplinary, with master's students Tools: Onshape, CAD Status: In progress — gripper rebuild Target: 7-DOF arm

01 · ITERATION 1

The first gripper

I'm rebuilding the arm from the ground up for industrial use, starting at the business end: the gripper. The first iteration was a single degree-of-freedom design — a geared linkage that drives two opposing jaws together from one actuator. It established the actuation concept and gave me a working baseline to design against.

This is a multidisciplinary project — I'm working alongside a group of master's students to build it. I lead the hardware and mechanical side, owning the design and physical build of the gripper and arm, while the software and control work is handled by the master's students.

02 · ITERATION 2

Three-point claw

The second iteration moves to a three-fingered claw that closes on three points of contact. Gripping an object at three points instead of two lets it self-center and hold a far wider range of shapes securely — round, irregular, or off-axis parts that a flat two-jaw gripper would struggle with. The fingers are still gear-driven from a single input, keeping the actuation simple while the contact geometry does the work.

03 · WHAT'S NEXT

Toward a full armature

  • Gripper — now1 DOF
  • Gripper — target2 DOF
  • Full arm — target7 DOF

From here the plan is to design proper housings for the claw's drivetrain and electronics, and to rework the finger geometry to make room for embedded sensing and actuation — building toward a smarter, self-contained end effector. In parallel I'm developing a second degree of freedom for the claw, giving it independent wrist-style motion on top of the grip. Once the end effector is solid, the build scales up to the full armature — a 7-DOF arm with the reach and dexterity of an industrial manipulator. This page will grow as each stage comes together.
