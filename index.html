About me

A bit about me

I'm drawn to the part of engineering where an idea on a screen turns into something that actually flies. Most of my projects follow the same loop — model it in CAD, simulate the aerodynamics and loads, build it, then work out what to improve on the next version. Right now that's a competition rocket airframe with the TMU MARS team and a payload-carrying quadcopter I'm designing from scratch.

Outside of coursework I've fabricated and assembled metal parts to tolerance on a production floor, and I run a small e-commerce business end to end — which has taught me as much about logistics and follow-through as any class. I'm currently looking for a Summer 2027 internship in aerospace or mechanical engineering.
